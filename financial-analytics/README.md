# 💰 Financial Analytics — Company Performance Analysis

**Dataset:** [Microsoft Financial Sample — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/atharvaarya25/financials)  
**Records:** 700 transactions · 5 countries · 5 segments · 2013–2014  
**Tools:** Python · pandas · matplotlib

## 🎯 Goal
Analyse company financial performance across time, segments, and geography.  
Identify growth trends, profitability, seasonality patterns, and business risks.

## 🔍 Key Findings
- Revenue grew 3.5x — from $26M (2013) to $90M (2014)
- Profit Margin stable at ~14.5% — company scales efficiently
- Government (46%) + Small Business (37%) = 83% of revenue ⚠️ concentration risk
- October peak ($22M) is 3.5x above monthly average — classic B2G seasonality
- 5 countries contributing equally (~$21–25M) — strong geographic diversification

## 🛠️ Methods
- Data cleaning: accounting format, $ symbols, negative values in parentheses
- Revenue and profit trend analysis by year
- Profit Margin calculation (Profit / Sales × 100%)
- Segmentation by client type and geography
- Monthly seasonality analysis

## 💡 Recommendation
Maintain Government and Small Business focus while developing Enterprise segment.  
Plan cash flow around October–December peak season.
